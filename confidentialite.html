Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how MONARK collects, uses, and protects the personal data of users of this site, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR | Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés).

1. Data Collected

As part of your browsing and orders on the site, we may collect the following data:

  • Identity: first and last name
  • Contact details: email address, postal address, phone number
  • Order data: purchase history, products viewed
  • Payment data: processed directly by our payment provider; MONARK does not have access to full banking details
  • Browsing data: IP address, cookies (see section 8)

2. Purposes of Processing

This data is collected for the following purposes: processing and tracking orders, managing the customer relationship, fraud prevention, improving the site and user experience, and, subject to your consent, sending marketing communications.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your data is based, depending on the case, on: performance of the sales contract (for order processing), consent (for the newsletter and non-essential cookies), MONARK's legitimate interest (service improvement, security), and compliance with legal obligations (invoicing, accounting).

4. Data Retention Period

Your data is retained for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, and in particular: order-related data is retained for the period required by accounting and tax obligations ([PERIOD — TO BE COMPLETED]); prospect data is retained [PERIOD — TO BE COMPLETED] from the last contact.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access: to obtain confirmation that your data is being processed and to obtain a copy of it;
  • Right to rectification: to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): to request the deletion of your data, under the conditions provided for by the GDPR;
  • Right to restriction of processing;
  • Right to data portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another data controller;
  • Right to object, in particular to processing for prospecting purposes;
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL) if you believe that the processing of your personal data constitutes a breach of the GDPR.

6. Recipients and Third Parties

Your data may be shared with the following recipients, strictly limited to their respective needs: our payment provider for processing transactions ([PAYMENT PROVIDER — TO BE COMPLETED]), our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER — TO BE COMPLETED]), and our delivery providers. These third parties are required to respect the confidentiality and security of your data.

7. International Data Transfers

As part of using our technical service providers, some data may be processed outside the European Union:

  • Hosting (Cloudflare, Inc.): a company based in the United States, operating a global network of servers.
  • Database (Supabase Pte. Ltd.): a company based in Singapore; our data is hosted and primarily processed in Ireland (EU), but Supabase relies on subprocessors based in the United States (including Amazon Web Services, Google, Cloudflare) for its hosting and support services.
  • Payment (Stripe, LLC / Stripe Payments Europe Limited): a company based in the United States, with a European subsidiary (Ireland); some data passes through US-based subprocessors (Twilio, Google, Salesforce) as part of payment processing and technical support.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): company based in the United States.

These transfers are governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, ensuring an adequate level of protection for your personal data.

8. Cookies

This site uses cookies to improve your browsing experience, measure site traffic, and, subject to your consent, for personalization purposes. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the consent banner shown on your first visit. You can also change your preferences at any time by clearing the browsing data stored by your browser for this site.

9. Contact

For any questions regarding your personal data or to exercise your rights, you may contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO) at the following address: contact@effluve-paris.fr
