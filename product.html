How to Wear
  1. 01 Apply to pulse points (wrists & neck) right after showering, while skin is still damp.
  2. 02 Don’t rub wrists together. It breaks the scent down before it has a chance to open.
  3. 03 Two to three sprays for daytime. Layer sparingly for an evening presence.
  4. 04 Reapply after 6–8 hours if the moment calls for it.
